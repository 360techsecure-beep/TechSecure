Common questions

Can every older system be upgraded to 4K?+

No. Cameras, recorder, cable connections, and available bandwidth must support the proposed configuration. We check compatibility before recommending an upgrade.

Can you keep my existing wiring?+

Sometimes. Reuse depends on cable type, condition, route, and compatibility with the new equipment.

Can I upgrade in stages?+

Where equipment compatibility allows it, a staged upgrade may be practical. Tell us your priorities so the quote can focus on the areas that matter most.
