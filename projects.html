Parking garage coverage

Camera placement around concrete columns and vehicle lanes

The photos show turret cameras mounted near structural columns and beams in a parking garage. These positions can keep the camera housing clear of vehicle traffic while giving the lens an open view across approach lanes and shared areas.

Planning considerations

  • Use columns and beams without blocking the intended field of view.
  • Follow existing conduit routes where appropriate and protect exposed cable.
  • Check lighting transitions, parked-vehicle obstructions, and approach angles.

Shared building areas

Coverage for entrances, corridors, reception, and service rooms

The photos show cameras in common spaces including a reception area, corridor, and laundry-room entry. Shared buildings often need several viewpoints because walls, doorways, and changes in ceiling height divide the space.

Planning considerations

  • Prioritize entrances, circulation paths, and shared service areas.
  • Use overlapping views where a single camera would leave a blind corner.
  • Plan who can access recordings and where the recorder will be secured.
