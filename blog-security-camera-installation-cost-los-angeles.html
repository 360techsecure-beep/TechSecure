Security Camera Installation Costs in Los Angeles: What Goes Into Your Quote

The price of a security camera installation depends on more than the number of cameras. Cable routes, the detail you need to capture, recording storage, and the condition of an existing system all change the scope of the job. A useful quote explains those choices so you can compare the complete installation.

Tech Secure 360 provides free estimates for homes and businesses across Greater Los Angeles. This guide explains what to include in your request and what to look for in a proposal. It is not a price list: your property needs an individual estimate.

Start with what you need to see

Before choosing a camera package, list the areas you want covered: the front entrance, driveway, side gate, rear door, or a business's register and delivery area. Then describe the purpose of each view. Seeing that someone entered a parking lot is a different requirement from identifying a face at an entrance.

A wide view can help show what happened, while a closer view may be needed for useful detail. Camera position and lighting matter alongside resolution. Our camera placement guide is a starting point for planning those views.

What changes the installation quote?

  • Camera count and capabilities. Outdoor exposure, lighting, viewing distance, and the detail required affect equipment selection. Ask for model numbers so you can compare the proposed cameras.
  • Cable routes and access. Accessible cable paths and short runs create a different job from long exterior routes, multiple floors, or areas that need conduit. Ask how wiring will be supported, protected, and finished.
  • Existing equipment. A compatible, working cable run may be reusable. An older cable or connector may need repair or replacement. Compatibility should be checked before assuming an upgrade will reuse everything.
  • Recording and storage. The recorder, storage capacity, recording settings, and number of cameras all affect the system design. Ask what recording history the proposed configuration is intended to provide.
  • Network and phone access. Include any required switches, power supplies, network work, and remote-viewing setup in the scope. Clarify who will own the administrator account after handover.
  • Installation conditions. Work at height, restricted access, business operating hours, and coordination with a property manager can affect how the job is scheduled and carried out.

Compare complete scopes, not just the total price

Two quotes for four cameras may cover very different work. One may include the recorder and storage, protected cable runs, phone setup, and a walkthrough. Another may be for labor only. Ask each installer to put the following in writing:

  • Camera and recorder models, quantities, and storage capacity.
  • Planned camera locations and what each view should capture.
  • Included cabling, conduit, connectors, power supplies, and network equipment.
  • Whether the quote includes equipment, labor, taxes, removal of old equipment, and any finishing work.
  • Recording settings, phone access, account handover, and user training.
  • Equipment and workmanship warranty terms, exclusions, and the process for requesting support.
  • Any optional services or recurring charges.

Do you need to replace the whole system?

If you already have cameras, explain what is wrong before requesting a replacement. Is one view blurry, are multiple cameras offline, or has the recorder stopped saving footage? Those symptoms call for different checks.

Tech Secure 360 offers repairs and upgrades as well as new installations. Ask whether a targeted repair or partial upgrade would meet your needs, and what limitations would remain. Our guide to upgrading an older camera system covers the questions to consider.

Ask about recording fees and storage

Tech Secure 360 offers systems that record locally without required monthly recording fees. Optional cloud backup is a separate choice. Confirm exactly what the quoted system includes and whether any optional features carry ongoing costs.

Storage duration depends on the system's recording settings and capacity. For example, Ubiquiti's storage documentation explains that retention can be managed through storage and per-camera settings. Controls vary by manufacturer; ask your installer to demonstrate the settings on your actual recorder. See our comparison of cloud and local storage for the basic trade-offs.

What to send for a useful estimate

Include your neighborhood, property type, the areas you want covered, and whether you already have a system. Photos of the proposed camera locations and existing recorder can help explain the job. A model number is useful; passwords and account credentials are not needed for an estimate request.

For a business or apartment building, mention access restrictions and who will coordinate the installation. If there is a specific problem, such as a blind spot at the entrance or missing recordings, describe it directly.
