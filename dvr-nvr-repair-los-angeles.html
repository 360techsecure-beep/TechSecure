How we can help

Recording problems

Missing playback, storage errors, or a recorder that stops saving video. We check the recorder, storage configuration, and connected equipment.

Offline camera channels

Blank feeds or intermittent connections can involve camera power, cabling, PoE equipment, or network settings. We investigate the affected parts of the system.

Phone viewing problems

If the cameras work on your monitor but not your phone, we review the app and network connection as part of troubleshooting.

Common questions

Do I need to replace every camera?+

Not necessarily. The recommendation depends on the fault and compatibility of your existing cameras, recorder, and wiring.

Can you recover missing footage?+

Repairing a recorder does not guarantee recovery of missing or overwritten video. Tell us before service if preserving existing recordings is important.

What does repair cost?+

The cost depends on the fault, equipment, and access needed. Request a free estimate and describe the symptoms; diagnostic or repair work is quoted separately.
