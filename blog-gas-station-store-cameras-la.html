Security Cameras for Gas Stations & Convenience Stores in LA

Few businesses are as camera-dependent as a gas station or convenience store. You've got cash, high foot traffic, fuel theft, drive-offs, late hours, and a constant stream of strangers — plus insurers and, often, franchise requirements expecting solid surveillance. Here's what effective coverage looks like for an LA fuel or convenience business.

The zones that actually matter

  • Registers and back counter — the highest priority. You want faces and hand-level detail at every point of sale.
  • Entrances and exits — a clean, well-lit shot of everyone coming and going.
  • Fuel pumps and forecourt — to catch drive-offs, disputes, and card-skimmer tampering, and to read plates.
  • Coolers and high-theft aisles — alcohol, tobacco, and grab-and-go items walk out fast.
  • Parking lot and perimeter — loitering, vandalism, and after-hours activity.
  • Back office and safe — for cash-handling accountability.

Detail beats camera count

A dozen cameras that produce a blurry smear are worse than a handful placed and specced correctly. License plates and faces need enough resolution and the right angle and lighting to be usable in an investigation — our resolution guide explains where the megapixels actually help. A camera aimed too high catches the tops of heads and reads no plates at all.

Lighting and night performance

These businesses run late, so night footage isn't optional. Bright, even forecourt lighting plus proper low-light cameras is the difference between identifying someone and shrugging at a dark blob. See getting usable footage after dark.

Storage, retention, and access

High-traffic sites generate a lot of video, and you may need to hold footage for a set period for insurance, franchise, or dispute reasons. Plan storage and retention deliberately — our take on cloud vs. local storage helps — and set up remote access so an owner can check any site from anywhere.

Consider access control

For back rooms, offices, and after-hours entry, cameras pair naturally with access control so you know not just what happened but who opened what. We cover the basics in access control for LA businesses.

Let's scope your site

Every station and store lays out differently, and coverage that works for one won't map cleanly onto another.
